# Changelog

Module versions go into RNG seeds: a major bump means existing seeds regenerate differently for that module.

## Unreleased

- The party (`src/ui/party.ts`): right-click the map (or ⚑ then click) to put the party somewhere; the marker is saved (`party` in the save file) and moves without regenerating anything. The Nearby page reports from there: the place, who's in each building close by and what they're doing, who's out and about, the talk heard round about, visitors in town, and work going nearby. "Who do they bump into?" rolls a real person out and about near the marker, with looks, voice, habit, want and an opening line by how they take to strangers. Deeds: log what the party did and it spreads from that building like any news (`deed` events, `deedsIn` in gossip), in three tellings, the third blaming a local; "who knows?" works on them; the town's name for the party goes in every telling. The Jobs page: debts to collect, rivals to bring low, proof to find, letters to carry, remedies to fetch, pilgrims to escort, houses to guard and thieves to find, each with a patron who can pay, a fee, the risk and where to ask.

- Town plans (`settings.design`, the Plan menu): walls (none, palisade, stone), gates 2-6, shape (round, oval, long along the high street), castle yes/no, market small/large, suburbs few/many. Each is rolled as before when left out, from the same draws, so towns without a plan are unchanged. Plans ride in saves (`town.config.design`) and the URL. Hamlets ignore them. A layout retry now also brings suburb folk inside the walls, so a two-gate city places every building; rivers are kept clear of the whole outline, not just the radius at their own angle (town snapshot updated).
- character 1.0.0: every person has what they want and fear, how they talk, a habit, a knack, how they take to strangers, faith, health, letters, tongues, and what they carry (coin from the household's income, and a pocketful of things). All rerollable and editable. A Secrets panel shows what they hide, what they were told, and the talk that has reached them, nearest to home first (`secretsOf` in gossip).
- Routines: errands to real shops (baker, butcher, mill, smithy, chandler, cobbler and the rest, by what the town has and the household can afford), the market on market days, and evening visits to friends, kin and sweethearts, in their homes. Their own streams, so nobody else's day moves.
- History: two lines with pronoun slips fixed ("the street he still lives in"; "people say").


- Populations: hamlet and village house sizes (`avgSize` in uses.yaml) are now measured values; farmhouses were planned at 5.5 people and held 4.5, so hamlets came out 8-13% short. Now within about 5% on average at every size. Street plans that ran out of plots used to drop buildings silently (up to 8% of a town's people); the layout now tries again with more room (`room` in `TownOpts`), so every planned building is placed. Towns that already fit are unchanged.
- Layout: towns are more irregular ovals rather than near-circles (stronger outline wobble). Hamlet layout is about 20 times faster at 600 people (distance checks against roads prune by bounding box; `nearLine`), with identical output.
- Gossip: chatter picks the kind of talk before the pair, so friendships no longer crowd out everything else, and each kind has several wordings.
- UI: "Your towns" keeps every town you have edited, pinned in or noted, so generating a new seed no longer loses one. "Here now" lists people by household. A Print button prints the inspector with all sections open and a map inset. The town's name goes where it covers the fewest roads and roofs.
- Serving: `town-gen-serve` runs `packaging/serve.py`, which revalidates the page on every load and caches hashed assets, so a rebuild never leaves a stale page. `tools/look.mjs` finds chromium-browser (Fedora) or takes `CHROME`.
- Golden and town snapshots updated (pre-release).

- UI: ways into the town (`src/ui/discover.ts`). "Who knows?" on every relationship and rumour: those in it, those who kept it, and everyone who has heard, earliest first, each with where they are now and later. Map lenses: wealth, class, parishes, ward value, guilds, and threads from the selected person to everyone they are tied to. Find: search by name, trade, building or ward, and lists (richest, debts, secrets and when they come out, feuds, offices, the oldest, trades). The almanac: feasts, quarter-days, visitors and secrets coming out over the next sixty days. Follow a person: their day drawn on the map, with who else is at each stop. `occupancy()` is memoised.

- Layout: copies of the same use spread out across the town instead of filling neighbouring plots (drinking houses and food shops count as one family); butchers, fishmongers, poulterers, mercers, drapers, goldsmiths and money changers keep together instead (`cluster` in urban.yaml). Civic: watch sergeants are appointed first, so nobody holds two offices. Town snapshot updated.

- Milestone 8: gossip 1.0.0 (secrets come out, news and chatter, spread at a block an hour through taverns, three tellings, the people it's about hear last, `knownByAt`; three sifters). Panel rows can light up buildings on the map. Content expanded across the existing tables (temperaments, looks, histories, names, debts, rivalries, disputes, decrees, living conditions, town matters, misdeeds, guild rules). Golden and town snapshots updated (pre-release).

- Milestone 7: towns. Street-plan layout for settlements over 700 people (walls, gates, wards with value classes, burgage plots, churchyards, castle, suburbs, river, quay). Urban building plan over 1,000 people. New modules religion 1.0.0, civic 1.0.0, guilds 1.0.0, lodging 1.0.0; town routines, living conditions, histories and visitors (local fair, assizes). Engine: `onWardResolved`, an edge index, `dmath.atan2`. Hamlets unchanged (the golden snapshot didn't move).

- Milestone 6: manor 1.0.0 (manorial villages: the lord, steward, regarder, bailiff and reeve; villein, cottar, free and commuted households; week-work and boon-work; the socman; runaways; bailiff visits and manor courts; four sifters). Visitors take extra sources through a `visitSources` registry. Free settlements are unchanged (the golden snapshot didn't move).
